About

I'm Saloni Kothari, a designer focused on healthcare. I like the hard, tangled problems — where policy, data, and real human needs collide — and I work to make them legible and actionable through design.

My work spans data visualization, systems thinking, and interactive storytelling. Whatever the medium, the goal is the same: help people understand a problem well enough to act on it.
