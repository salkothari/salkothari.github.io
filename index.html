About

I grew up in Bangkok, drawn equally to painting and science, and I've been connecting the two ever since, translating complex medical ideas into form, color, and texture that cross barriers of language and literacy.

For three years I've illustrated for Médecins Sans Frontières, advocating for affordable medicines and diagnostics. A diagram I made on tuberculosis drug pricing reached 1.3 million people through Vlogbrothers; two months later, Johnson & Johnson dropped its patents across 134 lower-income countries.

I studied at UCLA, completed a Media, Medicine & Health capstone at Harvard, and have since lived in London. Alongside design, I write op-eds and essays on subjects ranging from women's health and faith to insomnia.
